# Customer sign-in

Customers don't need a password. They sign in with their mobile number and a 6-digit SMS code.

## How it works for the customer

1. Press **Sign in with SMS** (on the shop) or enter their mobile on the order page.
2. Press **Send code**.
3. Type the 6-digit code — it **signs in automatically** as soon as the 6th digit is entered (iPhones can fill it in from the text). The only button shown is **Verify**.
4. Didn't get it? A small **Resend code** link counts down and can be pressed after **60 seconds**. The system also refuses a second code to the same number within 60 seconds — this saves SMS credits.

## After signing in

- **My orders** — every order on that mobile number, with readable dates.
- **View order** on each one — opens the order docket.
- **Order again** — puts last time's items into the cart on your **designed shop page**, so they can review weights and check out. Stock items (hams, turkeys) are skipped with a note asking them to choose from what's in stock.

## Good to know

- Phone numbers match with or without spaces.
- Codes are sent by SMS and use credits (see **Messaging → SMS usage**).

## FAQ

**The customer didn't get a code.** Check they typed the right mobile, wait 60 seconds and use Resend. Or send them their order link directly: open the order → **Send order**.
