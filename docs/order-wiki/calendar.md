# Pickup Calendar

A month view of your pickup dates.

## What each day shows

- Number of orders collecting that day.
- Deposits collected and the known value of the orders.
- Capacity used (if a daily limit is set).
- Whether the date is locked.

## How to

- Click a day to open it in **Pickup Day View**.
- Press **Refresh** after big changes.

## Good to know

- Orders given a pickup with **Other date…** (outside your normal pickup range) show on that day too, so the butchers know to have them ready.
- Orders with no pickup date don't appear on the calendar — open them and set one.

## FAQ

**An early pickup isn't showing.** Open the order and make sure the pickup date is set (use Other date… if it's outside the normal list).
