# Menu Layouts

**Settings → Menu Layouts** controls how products are laid out.

## Options

- **Customer shop layout:**
  - **Fast order** — quantity and weight on one page, add with one tap (recommended).
  - **Traditional shop** — tap a product to open it, then set quantity/weight.
- **Category chips:** *Category view only* (recommended) or *Show "All" + every category*.
- **Default starting category:** which category opens first.
- **Shop look & feel:** catalogue width, padding, card corners, and colours (accent, card, border, text, muted text, buttons, inputs, page background).
- **Staff New Order layout:** fast or traditional for staff.

## Good to know

- Your shop page on your website may use the **slim (editorial) layout** chosen in the website editor; colours there follow your website theme.
- Changes show in the shop straight away (within about 15 seconds).
