# The customer shop

Customers order from the shop page on your own website (for example your "online butcher" page). It uses the slim, clean layout designed for your site.

## What customers do

1. **Browse** by category. Each product shows its price (per kg or each).
2. **Specials** tab — products on special, with the old price and the saving.
3. **Add to cart:**
   - Weighed items: enter roughly how much (kg) — it starts at the minimum.
   - Items by the piece: choose how many.
   - Options (glaze, stuffing…) and notes where offered.
   - **Stock items (hams, turkeys) when stock control is on:** they see "Choose yours — exact weight and price" and tap the actual piece, for example **5.44 kg · $117.23**. Each piece can only be bought once. Sizes with nothing left show **Sold out**.
4. **Your order** (the cart) shows each item with its full name (for example "TURKEY – Whole 5–5.8 kg"), estimated or exact prices, and the deposit due.
5. **Checkout:** first name, last name, mobile, email, pickup day and time window.
6. **Deposit:** they pay the deposit by card through Stripe (if connected), or are told the shop will be in touch.
7. They land on their **order page** (the docket) and get a text/email with the link.

## Good to know

- Prices for weighed items are estimates until weighed; the final total is confirmed at pickup.
- If a ham they chose was bought by someone else before they checked out, they're told and asked to choose another — nothing is charged.
- Old links to the generic shop page automatically forward to your designed shop page.
- **Sign in with SMS** on the shop shows **My orders** and **Order again** — see **Customer sign-in**.

## FAQ

**A customer says everything in Turkey is sold out.** Stock control is on for Turkey and either nothing is received yet, or pre-orders are still waiting for their pieces. See **Stock**.
