# Order Rules

## Cutoff (lock) rule

- **Default cutoff mode:** *Days before* pickup, *Hours before*, *Weekday + time*, or *None*.
- **Value and time:** for example **3 days before at 17:00**.
- Products can override the shop's rule; the earliest rule wins.
- The **season lock** (Schedule) also applies — whichever is sooner.

When an order's pickup date changes, its lock time is worked out again.

## Customer editing

- **Enabled:** customers can use **Make changes** on their order page until it locks.
- **Change mode:** *Automatic* (changes apply straight away) or *Approval required* (changes go to **Change Requests**).

## Other settings

- **Business timezone** (for example Australia/Sydney) — used for all dates and locks.
- **Daily capacity limit** — the maximum orders per pickup day; full days can't be chosen at checkout.
- **System enabled** — turns ordering on or off for the shop.

## FAQ

**A customer says they can't change their order.** It's past their lock time or the season lock. Staff can still change it for them.
