# Change Requests

If **Settings → Order Rules → change mode** is set to *approval required*, customer edits don't change the order straight away. They land here for you to approve or decline.

## How to

1. Open **Orders → Change Requests**.
2. Read what the customer asked for (items added/removed, quantities, pickup date, notes).
3. Press **Approve** to apply the changes, or **Decline**.

## Good to know

- In *automatic* mode (the usual setting) customers change their own orders up to the lock time and this list stays empty.
- The customer sees "Change request pending" on their order page until you act.
- If an approved change adds a stock item (ham, turkey) whose piece has since been sold, the approval will fail — ask the customer to pick another.

## FAQ

**Why don't I see any requests?** Your shop is probably in automatic mode.
