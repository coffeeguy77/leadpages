# FAQ & troubleshooting

## Money

**Where's the money? Stripe shows much less than the system.**
Stripe only holds online card payments. EFTPOS, cash, direct deposit and contra are recorded in the system but never go through Stripe. On **Payments**, compare the **Stripe** total for the period with your Stripe account. See **Payments**.

**A customer paid more (or less) than the deposit.**
Open the order → Payments → Record payment and type the actual amount. If it was recorded wrongly, use **Correct** on that payment.

**How do I trace a direct deposit if there's a dispute?**
Search the Payments ledger for the customer or amount. Manual direct deposits record the payer name (as on your bank statement), bank reference and date received.

**A customer was charged twice online.**
Refund one in your Stripe dashboard. The duplicate is marked so it isn't counted twice.

**What are "Pending" Stripe payments?**
Checkouts a customer started but didn't finish. No money moved.

## Ordering

**Why is a product "Sold out"?**
Its category has stock control on, and either nothing is received for that size or existing pre-orders are still waiting for their piece. Give the waiting orders their pieces on **Stock** (Suggest matches) and the spare ones go on sale.

**The customer can't see Make changes.**
Their order is past its lock time or the season lock. Staff can still change it.

**The customer didn't get their sign-in code.**
Wait 60 seconds and use Resend. Check the number. Or send them their link: order → **Send order**.

**How do I set a pickup outside the normal dates (for example an early pickup)?**
Order screen (or New Order) → Pickup → **Other date…** → pick the date. It shows on the calendar and production lists for that day, and the lock time is recalculated.

**The price on a weighed item looks wrong.**
Check the line's actual weight and $/kg on the order. Weighed items stay **Est.** until finalised. Specials lock the price at the time of ordering, so an order placed during a special keeps the special price.

**An item name looks cut short in the cart ("Whole 4–4.8 kg").**
That was fixed — the cart, search and Specials always show the full name (for example "TURKEY – Whole 4–4.8 kg"). Refresh the page.

**An older customer doesn't want to order online.**
Take it in **New Order**, record their payment when it arrives (for example Direct deposit, $150, with the bank reference), then **Send order** so they have a printable copy.

## Stock

**I gave the wrong ham to an order.** Stock → Given to orders → **Release**, then give the right one.

**I typed a weight wrong.** Stock → the piece → **Edit**.

**A restored order lost its ham.** Restored orders don't get their pieces back — give it one again on Stock.

## General

**I can't see any orders.** Check the site picker (top right) and the status filter.

**Something looks broken.** Refresh the page first. If it persists, note the order number and what you clicked, and send it to your LeadPages support person.
