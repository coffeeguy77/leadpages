# Schedule & Opening Hours

## Pickup period & pattern

- **Pickup range start / end** — for example 15 to 24 December.
- **Pickup pattern:**
  - *Weekly*, *Fortnightly*, *Monthly*, *Custom recurring* — repeating pickup days.
  - *Fixed date range* — every open day in the range (typical for Christmas).
  - *Specific custom dates* — only the dates you list.
- **Opening hours** — the pickup windows for each weekday (for example Mon–Thu 7:30–6:00, Fri 7:30–5:00, Sat 7:30–1:30).
- **Always closed weekdays** (for example Sunday) and **Extra closed dates**.
- **Date overrides** — special hours or capacity for one date.

These dates are what customers can choose at checkout and what staff see in the pickup list. Staff can still choose **Other date…** for one-off pickups outside the range.

## Master lock date (season lock)

After this date **no customer can change any order**, whatever their pickup date (for example 15 December). Staff can still edit.

## Countdown

Show a countdown on the shop (eyebrow, title, intro text), for example "Christmas Cut Off — place your order by 15 December".

## Cart disclaimer

Text shown in the cart — for example explaining that weights vary and final prices are adjusted.

## FAQ

**A customer needs an early pickup.** Staff set it with **Other date…** on the order. Don't widen the whole range just for one customer.
