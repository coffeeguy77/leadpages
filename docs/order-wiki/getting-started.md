# Getting started

This wiki explains every part of the ordering system: what each screen is for, how to use it, and what to do when something looks wrong. If you have a question, search here first — most answers are already written down.

## Signing in

1. Open the **Orders** back office (from your website backend → **Orders**, or the Orders link you were given).
2. Sign in with your LeadPages email and password.
3. If you look after more than one shop, choose the shop from the **site picker** at the top right. Everything you see — orders, products, payments — belongs to the shop picked there.

> If you see "Your sign-in has expired — please sign in again", just sign in again. Nothing you saved is lost.

## Finding your way around

The menu on the left is grouped like this:

| Menu | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Today at a glance: new orders, deposits waiting, lock countdowns. |
| **Orders → Live Orders** | Every order. Search, filter and open one. |
| **Orders → New Order** | Take an order over the phone or at the counter. |
| **Orders → Change Requests** | Customer edits waiting for your approval (if you use approval mode). |
| **Order Operations → Pickup Day View** | Everything collecting on one day, by time window. |
| **Order Operations → Production Summary** | What to buy and prepare for a pickup date. |
| **Order Operations → Product Allocation** | Packing checklist, tick lines as they're packed. |
| **Order Operations → Stock** | Received hams, turkeys and other one-off pieces, given to orders. |
| **Order Operations → Labels & Stickers** | Print order labels and item stickers. |
| **Order Operations → Pickup Calendar** | Orders, deposits and locks by pickup date. |
| **Products** | Your catalogue: prices, sizes, options, specials. |
| **Customers** | Customer list, their orders and messages. |
| **Payments** | Every payment, with date range, totals and export. |
| **Settings** | Menu layouts, schedule, order rules, tax & payments, store setup, messaging, abandoned carts, import, and this **Help & Wiki**. |

## Words we use

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Deposit** | The amount a customer pays up front to secure the order (for example $50). The balance is paid at pickup once items are weighed. |
| **Lock / cutoff** | The time after which a customer can no longer change their order themselves (for example 3 days before pickup at 5pm). Staff can still change it. |
| **Season lock (master lock date)** | One date after which *no* customer can change *any* order, whatever their pickup date (for example 15 December). |
| **TBC** | "To be confirmed" — the price isn't known yet, usually because the item hasn't been weighed. |
| **Estimated** | A price worked out from the approximate weight the customer asked for. It changes once the item is weighed. |
| **Final / finalised** | The real price after weighing. |
| **Stock piece** | One received ham, turkey or similar item with its real weight, tracked on the **Stock** screen. |
| **Docket** | The order laid out like the paper order docket — what customers see on their order page and what staff see on the order screen. |
| **Over the counter / Remote** | How money came in: Cash and EFTPOS are over the counter; Stripe (online card) and Direct deposit are remote. Contra is shown separately. |

## Good to know

- Everything saves to the cloud. You can use the back office on a computer, tablet or phone.
- Customer links (texts, emails, QR codes) use your shop's own website address once your domain is live.
- Nothing is ever deleted when you cancel an order — it's marked **Voided** and can be restored.
- Every change to an order is recorded in its **History**, with who made it and when.

## FAQ

**I can't see any orders.** Check the site picker at the top right — you may be looking at a different shop. Then check the status filter on Live Orders is set to "Active".

**Who can use the back office?** The shop owner and anyone the owner has set up, plus your LeadPages support partner.
