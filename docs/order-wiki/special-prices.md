# Special prices

A special price changes the price of **one line on one order**. The product's normal price and every other order stay the same.

## Three ways to set it

- **New price per kg / each.** For example $30.80/kg → $27.00/kg. The line is still priced by weight when it's weighed.
- **Fixed price for this line.** For example "6 hams for $900", whatever they weigh.
- **% off this line.** For example 10% off. This includes options such as glazing.

A reason is optional, e.g. "bulk order" or "regular customer".

## How to

**On an existing order**
1. Open the order and press **Special price** on the line.
2. Choose the type, enter the price (or %) and an optional reason, then press **Save special price**.
3. To undo it, press **Edit price** → **Back to normal price**. The special price stays in the history.

**When taking a new order**
1. In **New Order**, press **Price** on the product row.
2. Fill in the special price before pressing **Add**. The cart shows a **Special price** pill.

## How you can tell

- The line shows a **Special price** pill, with the special price, the normal price, the reason and who set it.
- The order shows a **Custom pricing** pill at the top, and in **Live Orders**.

## What the customer sees

- Their order page, receipt and customer copy show **Special price**, plus the normal ("was") price by default.
- **Settings → Tax & Payments → Special prices** sets whether customers see the normal price at all.
- To hide the normal price on one order only (say the discount is small), tick **Don't show the customer the normal price on this order** under the order totals. They still get the special price.

## History — Customers → Special prices

Every special price that's set, changed or removed is kept, with the date, customer, order, product, normal price, special price, saving, reason and staff member. The history stays even if the line or order is removed later.

- Search by customer, phone, order number, product or reason. Filter by dates, or tick **Current only**.
- Click a heading to sort, click a row to open the order, and use **Export CSV** to download.
- Each customer's record (Customers → click a customer) also lists their special prices.

## Good to know

- Special prices are **kept when the item is weighed**, when the customer edits their order and when stock pieces are given. When weighing a special-priced line, the rate box is replaced by the special price.
- On a **fixed price for the line**, the customer can't change the quantity or weight online. They can still change options and notes, or remove the item. Staff can change anything.
- Deposits don't go up because of a special price.
