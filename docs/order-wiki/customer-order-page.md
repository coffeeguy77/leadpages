# The customer's order page

Every order has its own page (the link in their texts and emails, and the QR code on labels). It opens on your shop's own website address once your domain is live, so customers trust the link.

## What's on it

It's laid out like your paper order docket:

- Your logo, phone and email.
- The order number and order date.
- **PICK UP DATE** — day, date and time.
- **Surname / First name**, email and phone.
- The items: quantity, item, $/kg and amount, with notes.
- **Deposit** — for example "$150 paid" or "$50 due".
- **TOTAL PAYABLE INCLUDING GST** — filled in once prices are final.
- "For and on behalf of" your shop.

## Paying the deposit

If a deposit is owing and Stripe is connected, a **Pay deposit** button takes them to a secure card payment. Once paid, the page says "Deposit received".

## Printing

**Print / Save PDF** prints a clean copy — ideal for customers who want to bring a paper copy to pickup.

## Make changes

A quiet **Make changes** button sits at the bottom, **only until the order locks** (and the season lock). It lets them change quantities and options, add or remove items, and update pickup and notes. Then **← Back to order** returns to the docket.

- Stock items (hams, turkeys) holding a piece show "Exact weight 5.1 kg" — to swap, they remove it and add the one they want.
- After the lock, the button disappears; they need to call you.

## FAQ

**The customer can't see Make changes.** Their order has locked (or the season lock has passed). Staff can still edit the order.

**Can they get a copy if they don't use email?** Yes — staff can **Send order** by text, or print it for them.
