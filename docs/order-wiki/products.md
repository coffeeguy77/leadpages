# Products

**Products** lists everything customers and staff can order. Use **+ New Product** to add one, or click a product to edit it.

## Pricing methods

| Method | Use it for | How it's priced |
|---|---|---|
| **Per kg** | Most meat | $/kg × weight. Estimated from the requested weight, final once weighed. |
| **Each / fixed** | Pies, sauces, packs with a set price | Price × quantity. Known straight away. |
| **Estimated / From price** | Items with an approximate price | Shown as "Approx." or "From". |
| **Price confirmed later (TBC)** | Items you price on the day | No price until staff finalise it. |
| **Quote required** | Special orders | Priced by quote. |

## Size modes (per-kg products)

- **Variable weight** — the customer enters roughly how much they want (for example 1.5 kg). Use **Minimum kg** to stop tiny orders; the shop shows "KG (MIN 2KG)".
- **Each (individual item)** — the customer chooses how many (for example 2 half hams). There's no assumed weight: the line is priced when weighed. The docket shows quantity × $/kg.
- **Pack** — fixed pack sizes (for example 500 g pack). With a pack weight the price is known; without one it's priced when weighed.

**Quantity prompt** — the question shown above the quantity (for example "How many turkeys would you like?").

## Options (questions)

Add choices such as glaze, stuffing or slicing. Each choice can add a price (for example +$5 glaze). Mark an option **required** so the item can't be added without it, or **staff only** for internal choices.

### Cooking (charged per kg)

For a choice like **Cooked**, tick **Charge cooking rate** next to it instead of typing a price. That choice is then charged per kg at the shop's **Cooking rate** (Settings → Tax & Payments, e.g. $9.50/kg, GST included).

- Before the item is weighed the customer sees a range, worked out from the size in the product name. A *HAM – Half 3-4kg* that's cooked: cooking $28.50–$38.00.
- When you weigh it, the cooking is exact: 3.6 kg × $9.50 = **$34.20**, added to the line's final price.
- The rate is locked onto each order line when it's ordered — changing the setting later doesn't change existing orders.
- Tip: put the size range in the product name (e.g. *Half 3.5-4*) so customers see a price range.

## Categories

- Every product has a main category (Beef, Ham, Turkey…).
- **Additional categories** show the product in more places (for example a ham also under "Christmas").
- **Manage categories** to rename, reorder, hide or delete unused categories.
- Tip for stock control: stock control is switched on per category, using the product's **main** category.

## Specials

Edit a product → **Pricing** → **Put this product on special**.

1. **Type:** a set special price, or a percentage off. The editor shows the result, for example "10% off $50.00/kg = save $5.00/kg → $45.00/kg". It won't let you save a "special" that isn't cheaper.
2. **How long:** ongoing, until a date, or scheduled (start and end date). Dates are whole days in your shop's timezone; the end day is included.
3. Save.

While a special is live:
- The shop shows a **Specials** tab and a **Special** badge with the old price and the saving in the product's category.
- The staff product list shows **Special** (or **Special soon** for scheduled ones).
- **The price is locked in when the customer orders.** If the special ends later, their order keeps the special price — including when the item is weighed.

Specials can't be used on "Price confirmed later" or quote products. Turning a special off keeps the settings for next time.

## More actions

**Match categories**, **Apply weight & turkey rules**, **Deactivate unsized hams**, **Add Pies from titles** — tidy-up tools from the initial setup. **Unlock delete** must be pressed before deleting products (to prevent accidents); deactivating is usually better than deleting.

## FAQ

**A product shows "Price TBC" in the shop.** It's set to "Price confirmed later" or has no price per kg.

**Why does a half ham say qty × $/kg instead of a price?** It's an "each" item — the price is worked out once it's weighed.
