# Customers

## How to

1. **Search** by name, phone or email.
2. Click a customer to see their details, **order history**, payments and messages sent.
3. Edit first name, last name, phone and email, and save.
4. From a customer you can open any of their orders, or **copy** an order into a new one.

## Good to know

- Customers are matched by mobile number, so "0400 123 456" and "0400123456" are the same person.
- **Fix name casing** tidies names to "Given Surname" title case (for example JANE CITIZEN → Jane Citizen).
- Names are stored as first and last name; older single-name entries were split for you.

## FAQ

**A customer has two records.** They probably used two different phone numbers. Use the one they'll sign in with.
