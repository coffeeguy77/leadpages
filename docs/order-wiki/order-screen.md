# The order screen

Click any order (from Live Orders, the Dashboard, Payments, Customers, the calendar…) to open it. The order screen uses the full width of the page:

- **Left:** the order shown as the **docket** — exactly how the customer sees it on their order page — with the items editable in place.
- **Right sidebar:** everything staff need, grouped into cards.

## The docket (left)

- Order number and when it was placed, plus the **PICK UP** box with the date and status.
- Customer: Surname, First name, phone and email. A customer note shows underneath if there is one.
- The items table: **Qty**, **Item** (with options, notes and badges like *Est.*, *TBC* or *stock given*), **Rate**, **Weight** and **Amount**.
- Totals: known so far (or the order total once final), estimated total, deposit paid and the **balance**.

### Weighing and finalising a line

Items sold by weight show **TBC** or **Est.** until weighed.

1. Weigh the item.
2. Press **Weigh** on that line. Enter the **actual weight (kg)**. The rate is filled in from the order (in cents per kg) — leave it unless you're changing the price.
3. Press **Save final price**. The line shows the final price and the totals update. (**Re-weigh** lets you change it later.)

Stock items (hams, turkeys) given a piece on the Stock screen are already weighed and priced — you'll see the exact weight.

### Adding and removing items

- **Add product** (under the docket) is the same picker as New Order: category chips, search, qty and kg steppers, **Options** (with line notes) and **Add**. For stock-controlled items (hams, turkeys) tap the actual piece to add it at its exact weight.
- **Remove** on a line takes it off the order. If the line held a stock piece, the piece goes back into stock.

## The sidebar (right)

| Card | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Status** | The status and lock countdown. **Lock**, **Mark ready**, **Mark collected**, **Mark completed**, **Void order** / **Restore order**. |
| **Send & print** | **Send order** — choose SMS + email, SMS or Email, then send the customer a link to their order. **Print receipt**, **Print packing slip**, **Print order label**, **Print item stickers**, **Copy to new order**. |
| **Payments** | Deposit required and paid, and every payment on this order (amount, method, date, payer, reference, status). **Record payment**, **Correct** / **Void** a manual payment, **Send deposit link**, **Checkout deposit**. |
| **Customer & pickup** | Customer name (first/last), phone, email, **Pickup date** (the list, or **Other date…** for any day), customer notes. Press **Save changes**. Changing the pickup date recalculates when the order locks. |
| **Staff notes** | Notes only staff can see. **Save staff notes**. |
| **Importance** | Fold-out. Mark important, with a type (VIP, allergy…), colour and reason; optionally shown on labels. |
| **History** | Fold-out. Everything that happened: created, edited, payments recorded/corrected/voided, orders sent, messages, stock pieces given or put back — who and when. |

## How to

**Give an older customer a printable order after they've paid by bank transfer**
1. Open the order.
2. In **Payments**, press **Record payment** → method **Direct deposit** → enter the amount received (for example $150), the date it arrived, the payer name as it shows on your bank statement and the bank reference.
3. In **Send & print**, choose how to send (SMS + email, SMS or Email) and press **Send order**. (Or leave *Send the customer their order* ticked when recording the payment.) Their order page now says "Deposit $150 paid" and has a **Print** button.

**Change a pickup to a day outside the normal range**
1. In **Customer & pickup**, set **Pickup date** to **Other date…** and pick the date.
2. Press **Save changes**. The order appears on that day in the Pickup Calendar, Pickup Day View and Production Summary.

**Void an order**
Press **Void order**, give a reason, confirm. Any stock pieces go back into stock. Use **Restore order** to undo — restored orders don't get their stock pieces back automatically; give them again on the Stock screen.

## Good to know

- Staff can change an order at any time, even after it's locked for customers.
- Every edit is recorded in History.
- The docket on the left is the same layout the customer sees, so you can read it out to them over the phone.

## FAQ

**The order says "Deposit $50/50" but they paid more.** Correct the payment in the **Payments** card — enter the real amount.

**I can't see the Weigh button.** It only shows on lines priced by weight or TBC.

**On a phone or small screen** the sidebar cards move underneath the docket.
