# New Order

Use **Orders → New Order** for phone and counter orders — especially customers who don't want to order online. The customer still gets a proper order (docket) they can print and bring in.

New Order has three steps down the left: **Add Products → Customer & Pickup → Cart & Payment**. The **Client** card at the bottom left shows who the order is for and how many items are in the cart.

## Step 1 — Add products

1. Tap a **category** chip (for example *Ham*, *Turkey*, *Beef*).
2. On each product row:
   - **Qty** for items sold by the piece or pack.
   - **kg** for items sold by weight. It starts at the product's minimum weight.
   - **Options** opens choices such as glaze or stuffing. Required options must be chosen before you can add.
   - **Notes** for line notes ("cut thin", "no fat").
3. Press **Add**. The row turns green and the item appears in the cart.

### The same product with different options

Two whole chickens, one with Apricot stuffing and one with Italian, both boned and rolled:

1. Set up the first chicken (options, qty) and press **Add**.
2. Press **+ Another** on the same row. The first chicken stays in the cart and the row clears for the next one.
3. Choose the next options and press **Add** again. Repeat as often as you need.

Each one is its own line in the cart (remove any with ✕) and its own line on the order.

### Stock items (hams, turkeys…)

If the category has **stock control** switched on (see **Stock**), you don't type a quantity. Instead the row shows the actual pieces in stock — for example **4.78 kg #4 · $103.01**. Tap a piece to pick it (tap again to unpick). Pick two pieces for two hams.

- **Sold out** means nothing is left to sell.
- A note such as "3 existing orders are still waiting for this size" means pre-orders haven't all been given their piece yet. Give those first on the Stock screen.

## Step 2 — Customer & pickup

1. Search for an existing customer, or enter a new one: **First name**, **Last name**, phone, email.
2. **Pickup date:** choose a date from the list of normal pickup days.
   - Need a different day? Choose **Other date…** and pick any date on the calendar (for example an early pickup on 7 December). The pickup calendar, Pickup Day View and Production Summary all pick the order up on that date.
3. Add customer notes if needed.

## Step 3 — Cart & payment

Check the cart, then choose what happens with payment:

- **Send invoice / deposit link** — the customer gets a text/email to pay online.
- **Record in-house payment** — the customer has paid you already (EFTPOS, cash, direct deposit or contra). Enter the method; for direct deposits also enter the amount, date received, payer name and bank reference (see **Payments**). A receipt goes to the customer.
- **Pay later / no payment now** where offered.

Press **Create order**. The new order opens so you can check it, print it or **Send order** to the customer.

## Good to know

- Your draft is saved on this device. If you navigate away, it'll still be there when you come back.
- **Copy to new order** on any existing order pre-fills New Order with the same customer and items — handy for regulars.
- If someone else just bought the stock piece you picked, you'll see "That piece has just been taken — choose another". The list refreshes so you can pick again.

## FAQ

**The pickup date I need isn't in the list.** Use **Other date…**.

**The customer paid $150, not $50.** When recording the payment, type the actual amount received.

**The customer doesn't do online — how do they get their order?** After creating it, press **Send order**. They get a link to their order docket by text or email, which they can print and bring in. You can also print it for them (**Print receipt**).
