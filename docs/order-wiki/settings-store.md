# Store Configuration

General setup for your shop's ordering:

- **System enabled** — ordering on or off.
- **Industry preset** — the starting setup (for example *Butcher*), which seeds categories, products and message templates. Applying a preset doesn't delete your products.
- Order number prefix (for example ORD), currency and timezone.
- Pickup and delivery options.

## Good to know

- Your **Help & Wiki** (this page) also lives under Settings.
- Your logo, phone and email on the customer's order docket come from your **website settings** (logo and contact details), not from here.

## FAQ

**Can we pause online ordering?** Set *System enabled* to No, or use the season lock to stop changes while still showing orders.
