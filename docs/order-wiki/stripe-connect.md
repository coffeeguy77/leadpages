# Stripe Connect

Customers can pay their deposit online by card. That money goes to **your shop's own Stripe account** — never to LeadPages or Web Culture.

## How it works

- Your shop has a **Standard** Stripe account, connected to LeadPages.
- Card payments, Stripe fees, refunds and disputes all belong to your shop and are managed in your Stripe dashboard.
- LeadPages takes no cut of your deposits.
- Until a Stripe account is connected and able to take payments, the **Pay deposit** button is hidden. Customers see "Deposit is payable to the shop — they'll be in touch", and staff record deposits in-house.

## Connecting (one time)

1. **Settings → Tax & Payments → Connect Stripe**.
2. Enter the shop's email and continue. A Stripe page opens in a new tab.
3. **The shop owner** completes Stripe's onboarding: business details, ABN, the owner's ID check and the **bank account payouts should go to**. Make sure it's the shop's bank account.
4. Return to the Orders back office. The Payments card shows "Connected — charges enabled".

> If someone else (for example your website person) sets this up, the owner should still check the payout bank account in Stripe.

## Refunds

Do refunds in your Stripe dashboard (Payments → the payment → Refund). The order system doesn't move money.

## Good to know

- Every online payment is recorded automatically when Stripe confirms it (and re-checked every 10 minutes), so a customer is never charged twice for the same deposit.
- The Payments ledger shows Stripe payments with method **Stripe**.

## FAQ

**The Pay deposit button disappeared.** Stripe isn't connected or can't take charges yet — check Settings → Tax & Payments.

**Where do I see the money?** In your Stripe dashboard (Balances / Payouts), then your bank account on Stripe's payout schedule.
