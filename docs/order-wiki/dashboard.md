# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you see. It answers "what needs my attention today?".

## What's on it

- **KPI tiles** — counts such as new orders, orders awaiting a deposit, confirmed orders and orders locking soon. Click a tile to jump to Live Orders already filtered to that group.
- **Lock countdowns** — how long until orders lock for upcoming pickup days.
- **Recent orders** — the latest orders from the last 6 months (up to 12). Click one to open it.

## How to

1. **See who still owes a deposit:** click the **Awaiting deposit** tile. From Live Orders you can press **Send deposit reminders** to text/email them all.
2. **Open an order:** click its row.

## Good to know

- Archived (imported history) orders are left out of the recent list.
- The countdown refreshes by itself every 30 seconds while the Dashboard is open.

## FAQ

**A number on a tile looks wrong.** Press refresh in your browser. Tiles count orders by their current status, so a voided order disappears from "Confirmed" straight away.
