# Product Allocation

A packing checklist: every product for a pickup day, broken down by customer, so you can tick lines off as they're packed.

> **Not the same as Stock.** Product Allocation is about *packing* orders. **Stock** is about giving a specific received ham or turkey (with its real weight) to an order.

## How to

1. Choose the date and press **Load allocation**.
2. Work through each product group, ticking lines as they're packed.
3. **Print checklist** or **Print labels** if you prefer paper.
4. **Production Summary** takes you back to the totals.

## Good to know

- Ticks are saved, so another staff member sees what's already packed.
- The KPIs at the top show how many lines are packed out of the total.

## FAQ

**Can I undo a tick?** Yes — untick it.
