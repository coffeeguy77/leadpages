# Abandoned carts

## Automatic reminders

- **On/Off.**
- **First reminder after** — minutes idle.
- **Reminders per cart** — first only, or first + follow-up (after hours/days).
- **Max messages per customer** and **customer lookback (days)** — so nobody gets pestered.
- **Channels** — SMS, email.

The reminder links straight back to their cart on your shop page.

## The list

Carts that didn't become orders: who, what, how long ago, and whether a reminder was sent.

## Good to know

- Stock items (hams, turkeys) in an old cart may have been bought by someone else — the customer is told and asked to choose another.

## FAQ

**Can I switch reminders off for Christmas week?** Yes — set reminders to Off.
