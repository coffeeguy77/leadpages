Pricing questions, answered.

What’s included in the monthly plan?
Hosting, platform updates, included apps for your plan, SSL and Australian support. Exact inclusions are confirmed before checkout.
Is setup a one-off fee?
Yes. Setup covers getting your site structured, branded and ready to publish. The amount depends on pages, apps and how much content you provide.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. You can move up as you grow — for example from Starter to Growth — without rebuilding from scratch.
Do I need a custom domain?
Starter can launch on a LeadPages subdomain. Growth and Partner support connecting your own domain when you’re ready.
What’s the difference with Partner?
Partner is for people building and looking after client websites — with multi-client tools, care plans and partner support. Explore the Partner Program.
Are apps extra?
Core apps are included. Some are free to enable. Premium apps are optional upgrades when you need deeper capability.
Do you offer annual billing?
Yes. Use the Pay annually toggle above the plans. Annual billing is confirmed at checkout when available for your plan.
Who do I talk to if I’m unsure?
Start with Find a partner or email support@leadpages.com.au — we’ll point you to the right fit.
