FAQ

Questions before we chat?

How much does a website cost?

A standard website launch starts with a clear scope and quote. The final cost depends on pages, content, features and how much support you need.

How long does it take?

Most local business websites can be ready for review within two to four weeks once the key content, photos and approvals are available.

Can you rebuild my current website?

Yes. We can keep what is working, improve what is unclear and rebuild the experience on LeadPages.

Can I use my existing domain?

Yes. Your existing domain can usually be connected to the new website. I will help confirm the best way to manage the changeover.

Is hosting included?

LeadPages hosting and platform infrastructure are part of the ongoing website service.

Will it work on mobile?

Yes. The website is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile, with mobile treated as a core part of the build.

Do you help with the words and photos?

Yes. I can help structure your messaging and advise what photos or examples will make the website stronger.

What happens after launch?

You keep local support from Shaun while LeadPages provides the hosting, platform tools and technical foundation behind the site.
