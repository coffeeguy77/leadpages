Inter-Account Transfer Matcher

Load the unreconciled lines from each bank account — as CSV exports or as screenshots. This pairs the money leaving one account with the money arriving in another, so you stop bouncing between tabs to work out where it came from.

1  ·  Statement lines

Drop CSV files here — one per bank account
Most accurate option. Columns are detected automatically, so any Xero export shape works.
Drop screenshots here — PNG or JPG, one or more per account
Long/stitched screenshots are fine. Every row is read into a table you check and correct before anything is matched — nothing goes straight from pixels to your books.
