Questions about apps?

What’s included vs premium?

Many core sections — Trust Bar, reviews, bookings and portfolios — are included with your website. Premium apps like Online Quotes, Premium Gallery, SEO Command and Advertising are clearly marked before you add them.

How do I add an app to my site?

Log in to Manage, open Apps, and turn on the section you want. It appears in the layout zone designed for that app — preview before you publish.

Will apps match my website theme?

Yes. Apps inherit your theme colours, fonts and button styles so they look native — not like a third-party widget dropped on the page.

Can I try apps before paying for premium?

You can preview included apps on your site immediately. Premium apps show pricing and access details on their marketplace page before you subscribe or enable them.

Do partners use the same marketplace?

Yes. Partners browse the same catalog when building client sites — they pick the right apps per industry and hand off a site that already works.

Can I turn an app off later?

Absolutely. Toggle any section off in Manage without breaking your layout or losing your other content.

Do apps work on mobile?

Every marketplace app is built mobile-first — layouts reflow, forms stay usable and galleries swipe cleanly on phones.

Where do leads and bookings go?

Enquiries flow into LeadPages lead capture — email notifications and your Manage inbox. Booking CTAs can link to your scheduler or quote flow.
