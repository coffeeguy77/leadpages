Questions before you get started?

Do I need to be a developer?

No. LeadPages handles the technical foundation. You customise visually and stay the local face for clients.

How much can I earn?

Income depends on how many clients you build and support. Partners typically earn on setup and ongoing monthly care — amounts vary and are not guaranteed.

Is there training?

Yes. Short practical modules cover the platform, selling your first site, pricing, onboarding and support.

Who hosts the websites?

LeadPages hosts client sites. Domains, publishing and platform updates are handled in the partner tools.

Can I do this part-time?

Yes. Many partners start alongside other work and grow as local relationships turn into clients.

What support do I get?

Australian onboarding, sales guidance, launch help and ongoing platform support — you’re not on your own.

Do clients know about LeadPages?

You remain the trusted local contact. LeadPages powers the platform behind your work.
